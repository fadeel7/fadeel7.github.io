Subscription Billing Service

A full-stack subscription system on Stripe Checkout and webhooks. The decision that mattered: never trust the browser's success redirect as proof of payment.

Access is granted only on a signature-verified webhook, and the handler is idempotent — so a retried event can't double-grant. The subtle part was mounting raw-body parsing on the webhook route alone while the rest of the app reads JSON.
